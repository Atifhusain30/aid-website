05 / GOOD TO KNOW

A few things
worth knowing.

Real answers. No fine-print surprises.

Is AID available right now?+

Not yet. The app is preparing to launch. This website is a preview of what's being built, not a way to book service today.

How does payment work?+

In the app, you see a quote before requesting. A card hold is placed when you request help. The final charge is made on completion; if no driver accepts and the request expires, the hold is released.

What if I need to cancel?+

Cancellation is free before a driver accepts and during the first five minutes after acceptance. After that, a proposed $10 fee may apply while your driver is on the way, capped at the job price. The app shows the fee before you confirm. This policy is subject to final approval before launch.

How do drivers join?+

Drivers submit their documents and vehicle information for AID review before going online. Identity and background-check requirements are being finalized for launch. Driver registration is not open to the public yet.

Is AID an emergency service?+

No. If anyone is in danger or needs police, fire, or medical help, call 911. AID does not contact emergency services for you.
